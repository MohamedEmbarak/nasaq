01 / Identity

The name.
The whole identity.

The logo is نسق in Aref Ruqaa Bold, converted into editable vector outlines. The Arabic name stands alone, with no accompanying symbol or Latin lettering. Use the same wordmark in Arabic and English interfaces.

The wordmark

The Arabic name is the complete logo. Teal is the primary version for light surfaces.

One color

Use the ink version when the surrounding content needs a quieter identity.

On dark surfaces

Ivory and white variants keep the Arabic lettering clear on teal or ink.

Room for the lettering

Keep the name intact.

Reserve at least 12 units of clear space around the artwork on its 144 × 112 grid. Prefer 48 CSS pixels or wider for normal interface placement. The dedicated favicon is for tiny browser tabs.

Never mirror, stretch, rotate, or separate the Arabic letters. Do not add another symbol or English word beneath the logo. For accessible naming, give a home link a translated label such as “Nasaq home”.

02 / Color

Calm by nature.
Clear by design.

Deep teal gives primary actions a steady presence. Mint creates breathing room. Ivory warms the canvas, while ink keeps reading comfortable. Color earns its place by supporting a task.

Teal#006A64

Primary actions and the signature. White text: 6.47:1.

Mint#C8E7DD

Supportive surfaces. Ink text: 11.00:1.

Ivory#F7F5EE

The brand canvas. Ink text: 13.30:1.

Ink#142D30

Body text and dark surfaces. Mint text: 11.00:1.

These solid foreground/background pairs pass the WCAG 4.5:1 contrast threshold for normal text. Teal on ivory is 5.93:1. Test each new pairing, overlay, interaction state, and chart separately. Color never carries status on its own.

03 / Typography & voice

Arabic leads.
Both languages belong.

The logo uses outlined Aref Ruqaa Bold. UI text continues to use IBM Plex Sans Arabic and IBM Plex Sans, with their open, practical character. Give Arabic a generous line height and preserve its connected forms. Clear language is part of the identity, too.

05 / Motion

The name,
following the pen.

The lettering follows a reconstructed handwriting sequence: nūn descends into sīn, then qāf forms its head, bowl, and terminal return. After a brief pen lift, nūn receives its diamond dot. The exact outline, including qāf's incorporated end marks, remains intact. Each 3.6-second loop holds the complete name, clears, and starts again. Transparent GIFs come in teal and white; the static wordmark replaces animation when reduced motion is preferred.

Use the image as decoration beside a translated loading status. Keep the status in text so progress does not depend on seeing animation. See the reduced-motion usage example.
